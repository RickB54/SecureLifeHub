Here are the answers to your 4 questions (I put them in this text file so the IDE chat glitch wouldn't hide them from you!):

1. DO I LOSE MONEY IF I OVERESTIMATE? CAN I ADD MORE LATER?
No, you never lose your money! The OpenAI API works exactly like a prepaid gift card. If you put $10 on your account and the script only ends up using $6, the remaining $4 will sit securely on your account forever. You can always come back and add another $5 a year from now if you decide to add 100 new exercises to your gym. 

2. I CREATED THE KEY. HOW DO I ADD MONEY?
Perfect! Looking at your screenshot, you are on the right track.
To add money:
1. Look at the very bottom left corner of your screen (underneath "API Keys") and click the "Settings" gear icon.
2. In the Settings menu, click on "Billing".
3. Click the green "Add to credit balance" button to load $10 onto your account. 
*Note: Make sure "Auto-recharge" is turned OFF so it doesn't accidentally bill you again in the future!*

3. CAN I USE THE ANTIGRAVITY TERMINAL OR GIT BASH?
Yes! "VSCode terminal" just meant whatever terminal you normally use to run your project. Since you are in Vibe IDE, you can absolutely use the terminal right here!
1. Just open a new terminal tab inside your IDE (or press Ctrl + C in your current terminal to temporarily stop your "npm run dev" server).
2. Type "npm install openai" and hit enter.
3. Then type "node generate-openai-images.js" and hit enter. 
(Once it finishes, just run "npm run dev" again to start your server back up!)

4. WHICH IS THE CORRECT SCRIPT?
You want to use the file named: generate-openai-images.js
It is located right here in your project: c:\VIBE IDE Projects\secure-life-hub\generate-openai-images.js

Open that file, and on Line 10, replace YOUR_API_KEY_HERE with the new key you just generated!
